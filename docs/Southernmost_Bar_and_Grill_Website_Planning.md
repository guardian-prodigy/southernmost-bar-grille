# Southernmost Bar & Grill Website Planning

**Project:** Website Specification for AJL WebCraft

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Project Goal

Create a modern, tropical-inspired website that showcases Southernmost
Bar & Grill as **West Palm Beach's next favorite destination** for great
food, handcrafted cocktails, live music, and unforgettable island vibes.

The website should encourage: - Walk-in customers - Online food orders -
Table reservations - Event attendance - Repeat visits - Mobile users
scanning QR codes inside the restaurant

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brand Identity

**Style** - Tropical - Modern - Upscale but welcoming - Coastal / Island
inspired - Bright, colorful, sunset aesthetic

**Feeling** Visitors should feel like they're escaping to a tropical
destination the moment they open the website.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Homepage

## Hero Section

Large full-screen image or looping background video featuring: -
Restaurant exterior - Signature dishes - Cocktails - Live music - Happy
guests - Sunset / tropical scenery

### Headline Ideas

> Your Next Favorite Spot in West Palm Beach

or

> Island Flavors. Cold Drinks. Great Memories.

or

> Welcome to Southernmost Bar & Grill

### Subheading

Fresh seafood, premium steaks, handcrafted cocktails, live
entertainment, and island-inspired hospitality in the heart of West Palm
Beach.

### Primary Buttons

-   View Menu
-   Order Online
-   Reserve a Table

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# About Southernmost

Introduce the restaurant: - Caribbean & coastal inspiration - Fresh
ingredients - Family-friendly atmosphere - Great food and drinks - Live
entertainment - Perfect for lunch, dinner, date nights, birthdays and
celebrations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Featured Favorites

Highlight signature menu items with large photos.

Suggested cards: - Jerk Lamb Chops - Wings - Blackened Mahi -
Southernmost Burger - Seafood Favorites - Signature Cocktails - Key Lime
Pie

Each card includes: - Photo - Description - Price - Order button

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Digital Menu

The menu should exist as its own webpage.

Requirements: - Mobile friendly - Searchable - Organized by category -
Beautiful food photography - Easy to update

Categories: - Starters - Wings - Burgers & Sandwiches - Seafood - Island
Signatures - Tacos - Flatbreads - Desserts - Tropical Cocktails - Kids
Menu (if applicable)

## QR Code Ready

The menu page should have its own dedicated URL so a QR code can be
printed and placed on restaurant tables.

Example:

southernmostbarandgrill.com/menu

Customers inside the restaurant scan the QR code and immediately view
the menu without navigating the rest of the site.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Online Ordering

Allow customers outside the restaurant to: - Order pickup - Order
delivery (if available) - Add items to cart - Checkout easily

Include an "Order Online" button in: - Homepage - Navigation bar - Menu
page

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Live Music & Events

Dedicated section featuring: - Weekly live music schedule - Special
events - Holiday celebrations - Karaoke nights - Sports watch parties -
Seasonal promotions

Allow easy updates by staff.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Happy Hour

Dedicated promotional section.

Include: - Days - Hours - Drink specials - Food specials

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Drinks

# Gallery

# Overall Goal

Create a website that immediately communicates that Southernmost Bar &
Grill is one of the best places in West Palm Beach for great food, cold
drinks, live music, and a tropical island experience while making it
simple for guests to explore the menu, order online, reserve a table,
and stay connected.
