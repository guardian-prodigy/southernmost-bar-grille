Privacy

Your night should feel easy.
Your data should stay respected.

This policy explains how information may be collected and used across the Southernmost website and table-ordering experience.

Effective July 24, 2026

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the feature used, information may include:

  • Name, mobile number, email address and verification status.
  • Table number, seating area, assigned server, order history, special instructions, tips and tab status.
  • Reservation, billiards and private-event preferences.
  • Age confirmation for alcohol-related features, without storing a copy of identification unless separately required.
  • Device, browser, IP address, approximate location, referral source, interactions and diagnostic information.

2. How information may be used

  • Verify a guest and connect a device to a table tab.
  • Route orders to the correct kitchen, bar, table and assigned server.
  • Process closeout, receipts, refunds and service requests.
  • Respond to reservations, events, accessibility needs and support requests.
  • Prevent fraud, unpaid tabs, QR misuse and security incidents.
  • Measure site performance and improve menu, staffing and guest experience.

3. Payment information

When live payments are enabled, card and wallet credentials should be handled by a PCI-compliant payment processor. Southernmost should receive tokens, status information and limited transaction metadata rather than full card numbers. Payment providers apply their own privacy notices and security practices.

4. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The site may use cookies or browser storage to preserve a current order, remember an open table session, maintain accessibility preferences and retain a receipt on the guest’s device. Analytics tools may collect aggregated usage information. Guests can clear browser storage through device settings, although doing so may remove an open local session.

5. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with staff and service providers that need it to operate the restaurant experience, including point-of-sale, payment, SMS, email, reservations, analytics, security and delivery partners. Information may also be disclosed to comply with law, protect safety, enforce rights or complete a business transaction. Personal information is not sold for money.

6. Retention

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by data type. Browser-stored information remains until it expires or the guest clears it.

7. Your choices and rights

Guests may request access, correction or deletion where applicable, opt out of nonessential communications, decline optional analytics where offered, or ask a server to complete service without the digital ordering interface. Some records may be retained when required by law or legitimate business needs.

8. Security and minors

Southernmost should use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, logging and vendor reviews. No system is perfectly secure. The digital tab experience is intended for adults managing a restaurant visit and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as systems, vendors and laws change. Material updates should be posted with a revised effective date.
