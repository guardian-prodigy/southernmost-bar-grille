Accessibility Statement

Southernmost is committed to making its digital ordering and venue information usable by as many guests as possible.

Effective when digital ordering services are activated · Last updated July 24, 2026
Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceAllergens & AlcoholOrders, Cancellations & RefundsAccessibility Statement
Need help during a visit?

Ask a Southernmost manager or use the server-request control from an active table tab.

Digital accessibility goals

  • The website is designed for keyboard access, visible focus, screen-reader labels, responsive text, reduced-motion preferences and mobile touch targets.
  • The interactive menu book is supplemented by a searchable menu grid so the same menu content is available without page-turn gestures or WebGL.

Ordering alternatives

  • Guests who cannot or prefer not to use QR ordering may ask a staff member to take the order, provide the check or assist with payment.
  • When a feature is temporarily unavailable, the venue should provide an equivalent in-person path wherever reasonably possible.

Third-party services

  • Maps, payment wallets, reservation systems, delivery marketplaces and other third-party services maintain their own accessibility practices. Southernmost should select providers with strong accessibility support and monitor integration issues.

Feedback

  • Report an accessibility barrier to a manager during your visit. Include the page, feature, device and assistive technology involved so the issue can be reproduced and corrected.

Ongoing review

  • Accessibility testing should include automated checks, keyboard review, screen readers, zoom, high-contrast use, reduced motion and real mobile devices before launch and after material updates.
