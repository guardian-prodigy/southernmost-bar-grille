Orders, Cancellations & Refunds

How changes, cancellations, disputed items and refunds are handled for table orders and connected delivery providers.

Effective when digital ordering services are activated · Last updated July 24, 2026
Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceAllergens & AlcoholOrders, Cancellations & RefundsAccessibility Statement
Need help during a visit?

Ask a Southernmost manager or use the server-request control from an active table tab.

Before an order is accepted

  • You may remove or change items in the current order before selecting “Send order to kitchen.”
  • Once submitted, immediately use the server-request control or speak with staff. A change is not guaranteed until staff confirms it.

After preparation begins

  • Customized food and drinks generally cannot be cancelled after preparation begins unless the restaurant approves the request.
  • If an item is unavailable, Southernmost may offer a substitution, remove the item or issue an adjustment.

Quality or accuracy concerns

  • Tell staff as soon as possible and before leaving the venue. Keep the order reference or receipt available.
  • The restaurant may remake the item, replace it, remove it from the tab or issue another reasonable adjustment based on the circumstances.

Digital payment refunds

  • Approved refunds are returned through the original payment provider. Banks and wallet providers control when the credit appears.
  • Tips and service charges are handled according to the final receipt and applicable provider rules.

Uber Eats and other delivery marketplaces

  • Delivery orders placed through Uber Eats are governed by the marketplace’s checkout, cancellation, refund and support processes. Use the receipt and help tools in the provider’s application.

Chargebacks and unpaid tabs

  • Contact the restaurant and payment provider promptly before filing a payment dispute so the order can be investigated. Fraudulent disputes, intentional nonpayment and misuse of a table session may be documented and pursued as permitted by law.
