Need help during a visit?

Ask a Southernmost manager or use the server-request control from an active table tab.

Agreement and eligibility

  • By using the ordering service, you agree to these terms and confirm that the information you provide is accurate.
  • You must be legally capable of entering a transaction. Alcohol may be ordered and served only to guests who meet applicable age and identification requirements.

Table QR access

  • A table, bar-seat or billiards-station QR code is intended for guests physically present at the associated location.
  • Do not copy, alter, guess or misuse table links. Southernmost may require additional verification, move an order to the correct table, suspend a session or ask for in-person confirmation.

Opening and maintaining a tab

  • A mobile number or other approved verification method may be required before a tab is opened.
  • You are responsible for orders placed through your verified session until the tab is closed or reported compromised. Keep control of your phone and notify staff immediately if a session is being misused.
  • A tab may remain open during the visit. Southernmost may place a payment authorization, enforce a spending threshold, request a card in person or close a tab for security or operational reasons.

Orders and availability

  • Submitting an order is a request to purchase the selected items. Acceptance occurs when the restaurant or connected point-of-sale confirms the order.
  • Prices, taxes, ingredients and availability may change. Sold-out items, substitutions and preparation limits may be communicated before acceptance.
  • Allergy and dietary requests require staff confirmation. Digital notes do not guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Payments, tips and unpaid balances

  • You authorize Southernmost and its payment provider to charge the final accepted order total, applicable tax, approved fees and selected tip.
  • Tips are voluntary unless a clearly disclosed service charge applies. A chosen tip may be adjusted before final authorization where the payment provider permits.
  • If a digital payment fails or you choose to pay with staff, the tab remains due until payment is completed. Southernmost may use verified contact and transaction information to resolve unpaid balances.

Alcohol service

  • Digital age confirmation does not replace government-issued identification. Staff may refuse or stop alcohol service at any time.
  • Alcohol may not be purchased for a minor, removed contrary to applicable rules, or served to an intoxicated guest.

Conduct and venue rules

  • Guests must follow staff instructions, billiards rules, event policies and applicable law. Harassment, violence, fraud, tampering or unsafe behavior may result in removal and account restriction.

Service availability

  • The website, table ordering, payments, messaging and provider integrations may be interrupted. When digital ordering is unavailable, ask a staff member for assistance.

Limitation and disputes

  • To the fullest extent permitted by law, Southernmost is not responsible for indirect losses caused by service interruptions or unauthorized use outside its reasonable control.
  • Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot legally be waived. Venue-specific dispute and governing-law language should be finalized with qualified Florida counsel before launch.
