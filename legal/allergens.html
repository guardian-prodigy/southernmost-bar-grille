Guest policies

Allergens & Alcohol

Important information for guests ordering food, making modifications or purchasing alcoholic beverages.

Effective when digital ordering services are activated · Last updated July 24, 2026

Need help during a visit?

Ask a Southernmost manager or use the server-request control from an active table tab.

Food allergies

  • Southernmost kitchens may handle seafood, shellfish, dairy, eggs, wheat, soy, peanuts, tree nuts and other allergens. Shared equipment, preparation areas, fryers and utensils may create cross-contact.
  • Tell a staff member about any allergy before ordering. Add the allergy in the item notes, then request staff confirmation. Digital notes alone are not confirmation that an item can be prepared safely.

Menu descriptions and substitutions

  • Descriptions are summaries and may not list every ingredient. Recipes, garnishes and brands can change.
  • A requested modification may be unavailable or may change the price and preparation time. Staff may recommend a safer alternative.

Raw or undercooked food

  • Consuming raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish or eggs may increase the risk of foodborne illness, especially for guests with certain medical conditions.

Alcohol eligibility

  • You must be at least 21 years old and present valid government-issued identification when requested.
  • A digital checkbox, mobile verification or previous purchase does not guarantee service. Staff retain final responsibility for age and sobriety checks.

Responsible service

  • Southernmost may limit quantities, delay or decline an alcohol order, close digital alcohol ordering, or arrange staff assistance when responsible-service concerns arise.
  • Do not drive after drinking. Use a designated driver, rideshare or another safe transportation option.
