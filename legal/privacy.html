Guest policies

Privacy Policy

How Southernmost handles guest information across the website, table ordering, reservations and digital receipts.

Effective when digital ordering services are activated · Last updated July 24, 2026

Need help during a visit?

Ask a Southernmost manager or use the server-request control from an active table tab.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you choose to provide, including first name, mobile number and receipt email.
  • Visit and order details, such as table or station identifier, selected items, modifiers, service requests, tips and transaction references.
  • Technical data needed to operate and protect the service, such as device type, browser, approximate network information, timestamps and security events.
  • Age-confirmation status for alcohol-related orders. The website should not store photographs of identification unless a separately approved verification provider is implemented.

How information is used

  • Link a guest to the correct table or service zone.
  • Process, route and track orders and service requests.
  • Assign staff, provide transactional notifications and reconnect an open tab.
  • Prevent fraud, investigate unpaid tabs, protect guests and staff, and comply with lawful requests.
  • Improve menu performance, accessibility and the guest experience using aggregated analytics.

Payment information

  • Card and digital-wallet details should be collected directly by the approved payment processor. Southernmost should not store full card numbers or security codes on its own servers.
  • Payment tokens, authorization status, amounts, refunds and receipt references may be retained as needed to operate and reconcile transactions.

Sharing and service providers

  • Information may be shared with the restaurant’s point-of-sale, payment, SMS, reservation, delivery, analytics, security and hosting providers only as needed to deliver the requested service.
  • Southernmost does not sell personal information. Provider contracts should limit use of data to the services they perform.

Retention and choices

  • Open-tab information should be retained only as long as necessary for payment, dispute, accounting, security and legal obligations.
  • Transactional messages are separate from marketing messages. Guests should be able to opt out of marketing without losing required order updates.
  • Guests may request access, correction or deletion where applicable, subject to transaction, fraud-prevention and legal retention requirements.

Security

  • The production service should use encrypted connections, signed table links, rate limiting, audited staff access, tokenized payments and monitoring. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children and alcohol

  • The ordering service is intended for adults capable of entering a binding transaction. Alcohol is available only to guests who meet legal age and identification requirements.

Changes

  • This policy may be updated as providers, features or legal obligations change. The effective date shown on this page will be revised when material changes are published.
